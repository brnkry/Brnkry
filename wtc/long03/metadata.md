## Description
Letting your car idle for 10 minutes on a cold morning feels responsible. For almost any car built in the last 30 years, it does more harm than good. Here's why, what to do instead, and the different rules for diesel trucks.

In this video:
• Where the warm-up habit came from (carburetors vs fuel injection)
• What idling does to a cold gas engine: slow warm-up, fuel washing oil off the cylinder walls, a cold catalytic converter
• Is cold oil too thick? What the W in 0W-20 means
• The right way for a gas car: start, buckle up, about 30 seconds, drive gently
• Diesels: glow plug light, why long idling causes wet stacking and DPF soot, block heaters, and diesel fuel gelling
• Rules for everyone: clear all the snow, never warm up in a garage, don't leave it running unattended
