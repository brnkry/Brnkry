## Description
Your tire pressure light came on this morning, but all four tires look fine. Here's what's really going on.

You'll learn why cold weather triggers the TPMS light, what a flashing light means, where to find the right tire pressure (hint: not on the tire), and how to fix it in about five minutes.

🔧 What you'll learn
❄️ Tire pressure drops about 1 PSI for every 10°F
⚠️ The light comes on at 25% below the recommended pressure
🔁 Flashing then steady means a TPMS system problem, not a low tire
🚪 The right number is on the driver's door jamb sticker
⛽ Low tires cost you gas mileage and wear out faster
