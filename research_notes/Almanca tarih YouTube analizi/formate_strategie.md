# Formate und Produktionstechniken für Geschichts-/WW2-Kanäle auf YouTube (Fokus DACH, kleine Kanäle, 2024-2026)

Method note: research date 2026-10-20. WebFetch failed for every domain tried (DNS errors), so all findings below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Wording on official YouTube pages should be checked before relying on exact numbers. Long dashes are avoided in these notes on purpose (project rule). The format is "claim | [Source](URL)".

Channel context (from assignment): "Chronik der Macht", German, about 67 Shorts, views mostly 10k to 500k, best 923k (opened with colorized moving footage), a few long videos around 2.7k views, has trouble reaching 10M Shorts views in 90 days.

## 1. YouTube's own guidance and creator research: Shorts retention, length, looping, swipe-away, 3-minute Shorts, YPP thresholds

### Takeaway
Shorts can be up to 3 minutes since October 2024, and since 31 March 2025 a "view" is any start or replay, but YPP eligibility still uses "engaged views". The most important new fact: YouTube announced in August 2026 that from 1 February 2027 the full YPP entry bar doubles (8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views in 90 days), and Shorts ad revenue gets a rolling 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days gate. The 3M Shorts views route stays the entry to the lower tier (fan funding, Shopping). No rigorous public study proves 60s beats 90-180s; vendor data leans toward shorter Shorts for retention.

### Cited Findings
- Shorts maximum length went from 60 seconds to 3 minutes in October 2024 | [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/?p=666545); [YouTube Help: Get started creating Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070)
- From 31 March 2025, Shorts views count every start or replay with no minimum watch time; the old metric is kept in Analytics as "Engaged views" | [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2025/03/26/youtube-shorts-views-metric/); [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2025/03/27/youtube-changes-how-it-counts-shorts-views/)
- YouTube said the view-count change does not affect earnings or YPP eligibility, which still rely on engaged/qualified views | [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)
- A Short longer than 1 minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked globally (YouTube Help, as reported). Relevant for archive footage that rights holders (e.g. agencies, broadcasters) have registered in Content ID, and for music | [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)
- Current YPP (until 31 Jan 2027): full tier 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days; lower tier 500 subs + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days | [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-raises-bar-for-new-creators-to-join-partner-program-tightens-shorts-payout-threshold/)
- Announced August 2026: from 1 February 2027 new applicants for full YPP need 1,000 subs + 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20M qualified Shorts views in 90 days; existing partners keep their status (grandfathered) | [TechTimes, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323954/20260811/youtube-partner-program-doubles-entry-bar-adds-monthly-shorts-earnings-gate.htm); [Tubefilter, 10 Aug 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/); [streamer.guide](https://streamer.guide/blog/youtube-partner-program-2027-monetization-changes)
- New ongoing Shorts gate: channels must keep at least 10M qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days to receive Shorts Creator Pool revenue; falling below pauses Shorts revenue but does not remove the channel from YPP. Reports differ on whether this applies to all existing partners (one says yes, including long-standing partners) | [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/); [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-updates-partner-program-terms-shorts-payout-rules/585331); [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/323954/20260811/youtube-partner-program-doubles-entry-bar-adds-monthly-shorts-earnings-gate.htm)
- A YouTube VP justified the 10M floor by saying payouts below it amount to "a few cents" | [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-vp-cites-a-few-cents-to-justify-10-million-shorts-view-floor/)
- YouTube Studio shows "Viewed vs swiped away" (positive side now labelled "Stayed to watch") under Analytics > Content > Shorts; it does not mean completion | [vidIQ: Shorts algorithm 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide)
- Community heuristic (not official): aim for about 75-80% "viewed" vs 20-25% swiped away | [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-analytics-metrics-growth); [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics-guide)
- Todd Sherman (Shorts product lead): most Shorts traffic comes from the feed, not the shelf; people swipe "not knowing what comes next" (2023 interview, older) | [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/)
- Length evidence is vendor-grade and conflicting: one analysis says 15-30s Shorts get highest retention (often >80%); a secondhand figure (Social Insider 2025) gives ~50% average retention under 90s vs ~35% over 90s; a secondhand Vidooly study says 40-60s had the highest average view counts | [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention); [Bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/best-youtube-shorts-length); [Quso](https://quso.ai/research/youtube-shorts-length)
- Adobe Express survey of 507 creators: 53% said under 30s performed best for views (opinion, not measured) | [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/youtube-shorts-length-study)
- One creator source: Shorts of 60s or longer usually need 80-90% retention to perform strongly (unsourced heuristic) | [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/youtube-shorts-length)
- Metricool 2026 study (799,718 videos): Shorts feed generated 61% of YouTube views; viewers spent about 3x less time per Short; views up but watch time and ad revenue per view down | [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-views-are-rising-but-watch-time-and-ad-revenue-are-falling-study-finds/)

### Inferences
- With "views" now counting every start, the public view counter overstates what YPP counts. "Chronik der Macht" should check "Engaged views" in Studio, because that (not the public counter) decides the 10M/3M thresholds. Real qualified totals may be noticeably lower than the visible numbers.
- Timing matters: if the channel is not in YPP before 1 Feb 2027, the bar rises to 20M Shorts views or 8,000 hours. Realistic near-term target: the lower tier via 3M Shorts views in 90 days (about 33k engaged views per day), which the channel's current range (10k-500k per Short) can plausibly reach with steady volume. Getting into full YPP before Feb 2027 at 10M needs about 111k engaged views per day.
- Even inside YPP, Shorts ad revenue will need 10M qualified views per 90 days from 2027. For a German-language niche, long-form watch hours (8,000 from 2027) may be the more stable path; Shorts serve mainly as discovery.
- For length: since longer Shorts need very high retention and Shorts over 60s are blocked if any Content ID claim hits (common with archive footage and music), 30-60s should remain the default; use 60-120s only for story Shorts with a proven retention curve and fully cleared footage.
- Rewatches/loops now count as views; a seamless loop (last line flows into first) inflates visible views, but YPP uses engaged views, so loops help ranking signals more than eligibility (inference, unverified).

### Gaps
- Could not read YouTube's own Help pages (fetch failed); exact wording of "qualified Shorts views" and whether the 10M Shorts revenue gate applies to all existing partners is unconfirmed.
- No official YouTube data on optimal Shorts length or on how 90-180s Shorts are distributed versus under 60s.
- No YouTube statement found on how looping/replays weigh in ranking.

## 2. What successful history/WW2 Shorts creators do (first seconds, structure, colorized footage)

### Takeaway
No interviews or hard data from WW2 Shorts creators were found. Evidence is qualitative: colorized footage is widely used because it makes the past feel close and novel, especially for younger viewers, and broadcasters report rising demand. Industry hook advice converges on: strong visual plus spoken claim plus on-screen text in the first 1-3 seconds, frequent pattern interrupts, and burned-in captions for muted viewing.

### Cited Findings
- "World War 2 in Colour" channel describes itself as short military-history documentaries using the latest colorization; it substitutes similar footage when footage of an event is missing | [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCnWJY2kU6f6hXXL35KtSQgA)
- Long colorized compilations ("2 Hours Of Rare Colorized Footage From WW2", restored D-Day in color) are a common long-form format on YouTube | [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S0ENnZZ8zGk); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q6JRZKArHjg)
- Broadcast buyers' demand for colorized history surged; producers say many younger viewers find black-and-white docs a turn-off and color gives a fresh take on familiar subjects | [Broadcast](https://broadcastnow.co.uk/factual/colourisation-brings-history-to-life/5148429.article)
- Color makes WWI/WWII people easier to empathise with (opinion, 2019, older) | [Australian Geographic](https://www.australiangeographic.com.au/history-culture/2019/03/how-colourisation-of-historical-images-are-changing-our-national-identity/)
- Criticism: historians warn AI colorization/upscaling can be mistaken for accurate record (older, ca. 2020) | [Inverse/Input](https://www.inverse.com/input/culture/not-everyone-loves-that-youtubers-are-upscaling-historical-footage-to-4k)
- ARD broadcast a colorized "24 h D-Day" documentary, showing colorization is mainstream in German TV history too | [PC-Welt](https://www.pcwelt.de/?p=1192008)
- Simple History (animated, about 5M subs, ~20M monthly views in 2024) received a seven-figure investment from Electrify in April 2024; format is minimalist animation plus narration, mostly under 10 minutes | [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/04/16/simple-history-electrify-video-partners-investment/); [Electrify](https://www.electrify.video/news/electrify-video-partners-announces-landmark-investment-in-simple-history)
- Hook = everything seen and heard in the first 1-3 seconds; first visual, spoken line, caption and promise should work together | [Bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/best-hooks-for-youtube-shorts); [ShortsFast](https://shortsfast.com/blog/faceless-shorts-hooks-retention-2026/)
- Many viewers start muted, so the hook line should also be on screen as text | [GoFaceless](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/mastering-first-impressions-two-second-rule)
- Vendor claim: on-screen text raises watch time by 18% (Instavar 2026, primary report not found) | [GoFaceless](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/high-retention-youtube-shorts-attributes)
- Vendor claim: pattern interrupt every ~4 seconds held 58% average vs 41% for static videos (source/sample unclear) | [Aibrify](https://aibrify.com/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-curve-playbook)
- 3-second retention targets vary: 75-80%+ at 3s; weak curves lose 30-50% between second 1 and 3 | [Bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks); [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/p/scripting-youtube-shorts-engagement)

### Inferences
- The channel's own best performer (923k, colorized moving footage in the opening) matches the qualitative evidence: moving color footage in frame 1 is the strongest available "pattern break" for WW2 content in a feed full of static AI images. Recommend making "moving colorized/restored footage in second 0" the default, with stills only with Ken Burns motion and never in the opening.
- Recommended Short skeleton (inference from sources above): 0-1s moving footage + bold claim in text and voice; 1-3s stakes/question; then a new visual every 2-4s; payoff before the end; last line loops into the first.
- Maps/animated arrows are a cheap pattern interrupt and help clarity for battles; no performance data found.
- Colorization should be labelled ("koloriert") in description/on screen to manage authenticity criticism and the synthetic-content disclosure (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No interviews, analytics or case studies from WW2 Shorts creators (German or international) found; Weird History and colorization channels not documented.
- No quantitative comparison of colorized vs black-and-white vs stills on YouTube.
- No data on maps/animations performance.

## 3. AI narration, inauthentic content policy (2025), reused content and archive footage

### Takeaway
AI voices are allowed and monetizable if the video adds real value; YouTube's 15 July 2025 change renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" and targets mass-produced, template-identical videos, not AI as such. Archive or public-domain footage does not exempt a video from the reused-content rule, which is about transformation, not copyright. In the German market, AI history channels are criticized publicly as "KI-Müll", which is a brand risk.

### Cited Findings
- From 15 July 2025, YPP "repetitious content" renamed to "inauthentic content"; targets mass-produced/repetitive content, e.g. narrative stories with only superficial differences, slideshows with the same narration | [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/07/10/youtube-rolls-out-new-rules-to-curb-ai-content-who-is-affected-and-who-is-safe.amp.html)
- YouTube (Rene Ritchie) called it a "minor update"; AI tools are fine if content offers original commentary, modification, or educational/entertainment value | [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/youtube-responds-to-ai-concerns/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155)
- Reused content is a monetization rule separate from copyright; it applies even with permission; allowed when viewers can see a meaningful difference (e.g. critical commentary); clips edited together "with little or no narrative" are not allowed | [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en-GB); [vidIQ reused content guide 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-reused-content-policy-guide/)
- Third-party warning: archived news footage depicting violence may trigger enforcement if handled carelessly | [TunePocket](https://www.tunepocket.com/reused-content-youtube-guide/)
- A German historical-documentary producer says YouTube classifies excessive violence, inevitable in world-war docs, as not advertiser-friendly | [Der Standard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000287806/kigeschichtskanaele-verdraengen-echte-historiker-auf-youtube)
- Der Standard (via 404 Media): AI history channels with AI narration are crowding out real historians; listeners notice odd intonation and artifacts; one historian's channel plateaued | [Der Standard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000287806/kigeschichtskanaele-verdraengen-echte-historiker-auf-youtube)
- Synthetic content disclosure (since March 2024): creators must disclose realistic altered/synthetic content, including making a real person appear to say something (voice) or altering footage of real events; color adjustments and clearly unrealistic content are exempt; AI for scripts/captions does not need a label; repeated non-disclosure can lead to YPP suspension | [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/youtube-says-will-require-creators-label-ai-content-rcna143937); [Android Central](https://www.androidcentral.com/apps-software/youtube-ai-synthetic-content-disclosure); [Deseret](https://www.deseret.com/business/2024/03/18/youtube-ai-disclosure-labels/)

### Inferences
- An ElevenLabs narrator voice (not cloning a real historical figure) on an original script is fine under policy. The risk lies in sameness: identical template, same music, same structure every video, and slideshow-like stills. Vary visuals, add maps, original research angles, and keep scripts distinct.
- Cloning voices of real historical figures (e.g. Hitler/Churchill speaking invented lines) or AI-generated "realistic" footage of real events would require disclosure and is risky; colorization itself appears to fall under "color adjustments" (exempt), but AI-generated new scenes do not.
- WW2 violence and Nazi symbols: limited ads likely; blur symbols (the project's CapCut skill already handles symbol blur) and avoid graphic gore.
- For German audiences, a human voice (or a consistent, high-quality, recognisable AI voice) may become a differentiator as "KI-Geschichtskanal" carries negative press; no performance data to confirm.

### Gaps
- No data on whether AI narration lowers retention or reach on YouTube vs human voices.
- Could not confirm the current exact text of the inauthentic-content policy page.
- No source on how public-domain WW2 archive footage (NARA, Bundesarchiv) is treated by Content ID in practice.

## 4. Converting Shorts viewers into long-form viewers/subscribers; posting frequency

### Takeaway
Shorts are mainly discovery. The related-video link is the only native bridge; no official click-through data exists. Data from 18,000 channels shows Shorts+long-form and long-form-only grow similarly at small scale. Frequent uploading correlates with faster growth (vidIQ: 12+ uploads/month grow much faster), but per-Short performance peaks around 2-4/week, while total monthly views keep rising with more uploads.

### Cited Findings
- Related video link: one link per Short to any video on the same channel, set in Studio on desktop, shown as a play button; introduced 2023 when clickable links in Shorts descriptions/comments were removed (older) | [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-related-links-connect-shorts-long-videos/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-adds-shorts-links-limits-links-elsewhere/495463); [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2023/09/01/youtube-related-links-multi-format-creators-shorts-live-long-form/)
- Spotter analysis: Shorts posted within 3 days of the related long-form video drove more long-form views (single observation) | [Spotter](https://spottercommunity.beehiiv.com/p/increase-views-from-related-shorts)
- AIR Media-Tech, 18,000 channels (Mar 2025 to Mar 2026): at small scale Shorts+long and long-only grow within 2%; at large scale long-only pulls ahead (gap up to 90% at 10M-50M monthly views) | [AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/do-youtube-shorts-help-your-long-form-videos-grow-data-from-18000-channels)
- Metricool: around 5 Shorts/month linked with long-form averaging ~46% more views than the global average; benefit faded at 35+ Shorts/month; individual Shorts did best at 2-4 uploads/week, but monthly totals kept rising with more | [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/); [Metricool press release](https://metricool.com/press-release-youtube-study-2026/)
- vidIQ (5.08M channels, 2024-25 data): channels with 12+ uploads/month grew views ~8x faster than <1/month; newer study (10.2M channels, 2026): 4.1x median monthly view growth | [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/How-Often-Post-on-Youtube); [vidIQ Research](https://www.vidiq.com/research)
- Practitioner advice: daily Shorts for channels under 100k subs (Subscribr), start with 1/day and adjust (AIR); these are opinions | [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/how-often-to-post-youtube-shorts); [AIR](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/how-often-should-you-post-shorts-to-grow-faster)

### Inferences
- Long videos at 2.7k views likely suffer from topic/packaging mismatch with Shorts audiences. Make long videos the "full story" of the best Short topics, publish 2-3 cut-down Shorts within 3 days of each long video, and attach the related-video link to each.
- Series formats (e.g. "Teil 1/3", recurring named series) give viewers a reason to visit the channel; no data found, but consistent with the related-link findings.
- Posting: 1 Short per day is a reasonable test for reaching 3M/10M qualified views, but quality per Short must not drop (inauthentic-content risk and declining per-Short reach). Prefer 4-7/week with strong hooks over strict daily output.

### Gaps
- No official YouTube data on Shorts-to-long click-through or subscriber conversion.
- No German- or history-specific conversion data.
- No data on series/playlists formats for Shorts.

## 5. German titles, thumbnails and pinned comments

### Takeaway
In the Shorts feed, titles matter less than in long-form, but only about 40 characters show in the app, so the key word must be first. Curiosity/question and number titles are widely recommended; clickbait that over-promises is discouraged. No research on pinned comments was found.

### Cited Findings
- Shorts UI shows about 40 title characters in the app; put the important info first | [MiniTool (DE)](https://youtubedownload.minitool.com/de/nachrichten/die-besten-titel-fuer-youtube-shorts-schreiben.html)
- Max 100 characters; practical target 20-40 characters | [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-titles-thumbnails-matter)
- Titles, thumbnails and hashtags matter less for Shorts than long-form because the feed has no choice menu, but optimization is still advised | [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-titles)
- Curiosity and questions as key traits of good Shorts titles; numbers create clear expectations; avoid over-promising clickbait | [MiniTool (DE)](https://youtubedownload.minitool.com/de/nachrichten/die-besten-titel-fuer-youtube-shorts-schreiben.html); [Castmagic (DE)](https://www.castmagic.io/de/post/youtube-title-examples); [Whisper Transcribe (DE)](https://whispertranscribe.com/de/blog/how-to-title-a-youtube-video)

### Inferences
- German titles get long fast (compound nouns). Lead with the hook noun or number in under 40 characters, e.g. "Warum Stalin diesen Mann fürchtete" rather than a long descriptive title.
- Titles matter more on long videos, where search and browse use them; thumbnails for long-form should use a colorized face/close-up and 2-4 words max (general best practice, no history-specific data found).
- Pinned comment: use it as a question to drive comments, or as a pointer to the long video (since links are not clickable in Shorts comments, rely on the related-video link instead). No evidence found either way.

### Gaps
- No German-language data on title performance for history content.
- No research found on pinned comments' effect on Shorts or long-form.
- No German creator interviews on format (e.g. Mr. Wissen2go, MrWissen2go Geschichte, Terra X History) found in this pass.
