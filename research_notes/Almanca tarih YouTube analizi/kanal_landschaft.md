# German-language WW2 / 20th-century military history channels on YouTube (2025-2026 landscape)

Research note, 2026-10-20. Method limits: direct fetches of YouTube, Social Blade, vidIQ and similar stats sites were blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy refusal), so all numbers come from search-engine snippets of those pages. Every figure is tagged with its source and the date the source shows. No figure was invented. Numbers marked "undated" could not be pinned to a date.

## 1. Which German-language channels dominate WW2/military history, and how big are they?

### Takeaway
The top tier is public-broadcaster or broadcaster-funded: Terra X History (ZDF, about 1.36M subs, Jan 2026), MrWissen2go Geschichte (funk/ARD-ZDF, about 1.5M, undated), DER SPIEGEL / SPIEGEL TV (about 2.35M, unverified field), ZDFinfo Dokus & Reportagen (about 0.7 to 0.8M), plus WELT Doku and n-tv (no reliable numbers found). The independent mid tier is led by Militär & Geschichte (Torsten Heinrich, 300K to 344K, but mostly current military affairs/Ukraine, not WW2). Pure WW2 independents in German that could be verified are small (2K to 50K).

Independent / mid-size
- Militär & Geschichte (Torsten Heinrich, M.A. military historian, ex-soldier): site says over 300,000 subscribers, a video description says over 344,000; live streams with up to 20,000 concurrent viewers; focus now mostly on Ukraine war OSINT analysis rather than WW2 — [mgeschichte.com](https://mgeschichte.com/); [glaktuell](https://www.glaktuell.net/thorsten-heinrich-youtube-kanal-militaer-und-geschichte-ein-experte-analysiert-den-ukraine-krieg/); [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC9kZ6FlOQfusBV8LS2x2fAA)

### Inferences
- The German WW2 space on YouTube is structurally different from the English one: the biggest reach sits with license-fee funded (ZDF/funk) and publisher channels that recycle TV documentaries, not with independent creators like the English-language Simple History, Mark Felton or The History Guy. That leaves room for a mid-size independent German WW2 niche, especially for person-centred stories (pilots, U-boat commanders, tank commanders) that broadcasters rarely do as Shorts.
- Broadcaster channels earn reach via long (45 to 90 min) full TV documentaries; their marginal cost is zero, so a small creator should not compete with full-length "Der Zweite Weltkrieg Teil X" style series but with tight, character-driven pieces.
- Militär & Geschichte shows that the German military-interest audience is large (300K+), but it pivoted to current affairs; WW2 technology/biography content in German is under-served at the 50K to 300K level.

### Gaps
- No verified current subscriber counts for SPIEGEL TV, WELT Doku, n-tv Dokumentationen, Geschichte Easy, Generation Geschichte, Kurz & Bündig, or German Simple History dubs. The stats sites could not be loaded; search snippets did not contain the numbers.
- No per-video view counts for top WW2 videos of any German channel could be extracted (YouTube pages not fetchable). Report writer should treat "most viewed WW2 video" as unanswered.
- Existence/size of channels named in the brief ("Kriegsgeschichte", "Panzerdivision", "Die Geschichte des...", colorized-footage channels in German) could not be confirmed.

## 2. Which independent German creators post WW2 Shorts, and how well do they do?

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin. A German-language Shorts channel "Der Soldat spricht" (soldier-story Shorts) exists, and at least one faceless German WW2-facts Shorts channel using AI voice self-reports over 1.2M monthly views with 80% of the audience in Germany. No verified viral German WW2 Short with public view counts was found.

### Cited Findings
- "Der Soldat spricht": German-language Shorts channel with short videos about WW2 soldiers; voice type and numbers not visible — [YouTube Shorts tab](https://www.youtube.com/@Der_Soldat_spricht/shorts)
- A freelancer profile describes running a faceless German-language WW2 history-facts Shorts channel with AI voiceover and "high-quality visuals", self-reporting over 1.2M monthly views with 80% of viewers in Germany (self-reported, unverified; the profile appeared among search results from [LinkedIn](https://ru.linkedin.com/in/irinakhan) and [Collabstr](https://collabstr.com/abdulrehman95), exact attribution unclear)
- AI-assisted "cinematic reconstruction" WW2 Shorts with German titles exist from non-German channels (e.g. "Hier kämpfen keine Panzer… nur Menschen #shorts #ww2") — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UiYR8qhsbh0)
- Shorts overall reach about 70 billion daily views (industry claim) — [Submagic blog](https://www.submagic.co/de/blog/top-youtube-shorts-schopfer-die-dich-inspirieren)

### Inferences
- The German WW2 Shorts niche looks fragmented and mostly faceless/AI-voiced, with no clearly dominant brand. A channel with a consistent visual identity (restored photos, maps, neutral tone) has a realistic chance to become a reference in German.
- Self-reported 1.2M monthly views for a faceless German WW2 Shorts channel suggests the audience is there, but Shorts RPM in German is low, so long-form remains the monetisation lever.

### Gaps
- No public view counts for specific German WW2 Shorts; no list of German WW2 Shorts channels with verified subscriber numbers. Manual check on YouTube (search "#shorts Weltkrieg", "Panzer Ass", "U-Boot Kommandant") is needed.

## 3. Styles and formats of the successful ones

### Takeaway
Big players: full TV documentaries (45 to 90 min) with archive film, narrator and historian interviews (ZDFinfo, SPIEGEL TV, WELT); presenter-led explainers 10 to 20 min (MrWissen2go Geschichte, Terra X History); live streams and talking-head analysis (Militär & Geschichte). Small WW2 independents: narrated archive/map documentaries and faceless AI-voiced Shorts.

### Cited Findings
- Terra X History is co-produced by ZDF with objektiv media and is tied to TV formats — [Social Blade snippet](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/terraxhistory); [ZDF](https://www.zdf.de/unternehmen/dein-zdf/programm/public-value/terra-x-auf-social-media-102.html)
- MrWissen2go: presenter-led explainers, history focus from the founder's history studies — [deutschland.de](https://www.deutschland.de/de/topic/kultur/kommunikation-medien/held-des-internets)
- SPIEGEL TV: serialised long documentaries in playlists ("Der Zweite Weltkrieg" Teil 1..15, e.g. "Großoffensive der Roten Armee") — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uM__Ro48vaE)
- Militär & Geschichte: talking-head analysis plus live streams up to 20K live viewers — [mgeschichte.com](https://mgeschichte.com/)
- ZweiterWK: archive documents, maps and eyewitness accounts, non-sensational — [vling](https://vling.net/en/channel/UCzT2haIJWI-E__kVEn4FbyA/channel-info)
- Maximus and History: DDR archive film with narration — [vling](https://vling.net/en/channel/UC9ZXUWi74gxszqLBbRR9hIQ/channel-info)
- Faceless Shorts channel: AI voiceover — see Section 2 sources
- ARD's colorized D-Day doc ("24 h D-Day") shows broadcasters use colorization as a hook — [PC-Welt](https://www.pcwelt.de/?p=1192008)

### Inferences
- Colorized/restored imagery plus neutral narration is aligned with the broadcaster aesthetic German audiences trust; AI voice is common in the faceless Shorts tier, which may hurt differentiation as platforms label AI content (see below).

### Gaps
- No data on average video length vs. performance for German channels; no German creator interviews on AI voice vs human voice results.

## 4. Demonetisation, restriction or geo-blocking of WW2 content in Germany

### Takeaway
No documented case of a German history channel being geo-blocked in Germany for WW2 content was found. General evidence: war/violence content is often limited-ads or demonetised, the English "World War Two" series reported age restrictions and demonetisation, and platforms in 2026 are labelling AI content rather than banning it.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube ad suitability is first decided by algorithms using title, description, keywords and scanned video content; creators can appeal — [HAW Hamburg thesis](https://reposit.haw-hamburg.de/bitstream/20.500.12738/11140/3/Demonetarisierung_Youtube_Videos.pdf)
- Academic volume "Geschichte auf YouTube": depiction of war and violence triggers no-ads under ad guidelines; The Great War videos were partly demonetised "without recognisable pattern" (older, about 2018/19) — [dokumen.pub](https://dokumen.pub/geschichte-auf-youtube-neue-herausforderungen-fr-geschichtsvermittlung-und-historische-bildung-3110596822-9783110596823-r-6443450.html)
- The "World War Two" week-by-week series (Indy Neidell) was reported as increasingly age-restricted and demonetised (Sep 2023) — [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37372312)
- Platforms (Oct 2026) are moving to labels for AI-generated "AI slop" rather than bans — [etailment](https://etailment.de/magazin/2026-10-16-eher-etikett-statt-verbot-plattformen-und-ai-slop)

### Inferences
- For a German channel, practical risks are limited ads (yellow icon) on violent archive footage and swastika-heavy thumbnails, plus age restriction; blurring NS symbols in thumbnails and early frames and avoiding glorifying titles reduces risk. Legal geo-block risk in DE (StGB §86a) mainly concerns non-educational use of symbols; educational/documentary context is exempt, but YouTube's automated systems do not check context.

### Gaps
- No German-specific press report of a WW2 history channel being blocked/geo-restricted in DE/AT/CH was found; no German Reddit/forum threads surfaced in searches.
