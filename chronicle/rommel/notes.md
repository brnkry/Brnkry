## Fact basis (all from chronik/rommel/quellen.md)
- 14 Oct 1944: Burgdorf and Maisel arrive about noon. Rommel leaves with them about an hour later and dies shortly after (cyanide).
- Official cause: wounds from the 17 July 1944 strafing. State funeral in Ulm.
- 1917 Monte Matajur, about 9,000 prisoners by his own account; Pour le Mérite.
- 1940: 7th Panzer Division, "Gespensterdivision" (Ghost Division).
- "Desert Fox" came from the British press.
- El Alamein, Nov 1942: "victory or death" order, then retreat a day later.
- July 1944: he tells his superiors the war is lost. After the 20 July plot his name comes up. Choice: People's Court or poison.
