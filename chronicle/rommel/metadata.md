### Description
Hitler sent two generals to his most famous field marshal with a choice: stand trial, or take poison.

Erwin Rommel stormed Monte Matajur in World War One, led the "Ghost Division" through France in 1940 and became the Desert Fox in North Africa, so feared that the British commander told his officers to stop saying his name. At fifty he was the youngest field marshal in the German army. In 1944 he told Hitler the war was lost. After the July plot failed, his name came up. On October 14, 1944 he took cyanide. Germany buried him as a hero.
