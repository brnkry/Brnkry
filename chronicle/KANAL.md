## Channel description (paste into YouTube)
History told through its people. Chronicle of Power tells the true stories of World War I, World War II and the conflicts around them, through the soldiers, commanders, pilots and ordinary people who lived them.

Every video is built on original footage, period photographs and checked sources. No glorification, no propaganda, just what happened, why it happened and what it cost.

You will find:
• Short documentaries on famous and forgotten figures from every side
• Battles, campaigns and turning points of both world wars
• Myths and legends checked against the record
• Restored and colourised archive material

New videos every week. Subscribe and tell us in the comments whose story we should tell next.

Sister channel in German: Chronik der Macht
